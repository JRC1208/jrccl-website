Consumer Research & Fieldwork in Japan

Japan Research Consultants Co., Ltd. (JRC) is an independent marketing research company supporting Japanese and international clients with consumer research, respondent recruitment and fieldwork in Japan.

About JRC

JRC was founded in 1981 by Hiroshi Aoyama and has provided marketing research services in Japan ever since. Our primary focus is consumer research, and we support projects in Japanese and English.

Our main fieldwork base is the Tokyo Metropolitan Area. For projects in other regions of Japan, we work with freelance recruiters, interviewers and local research partners according to the location, target audience and requirements of each project.

Research in Japan

JRC supports overseas research companies and clients conducting consumer research in Japan. We can assist with local respondent recruitment, fieldwork preparation and coordination, interviews and product testing, with project communication and support available in English.

Our primary fieldwork area is the Tokyo Metropolitan Area, with projects in other regions arranged through our network when required.
